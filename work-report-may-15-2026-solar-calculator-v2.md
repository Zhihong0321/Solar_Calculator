- Identified the root cause for why adjusting battery efficiency barely changes the final bill saving: in the typical regime (night usage is the binding cap), `dailyBatteryDelivered` collapses to `nightUsage` regardless of round-trip efficiency, so only the export-credit component varies.

- Deep investigation: why dragging battery efficiency (Charge/Discharge Loss) from 0% to 20% barely changes the final savings amount. Traced the issue to three interacting factors in the calculation model: (1) the Export Saving Cap `Math.min(exportSavingRaw, afterBill)` which causes total savings to asymptotically approach the original bill amount, masking efficiency-driven changes in individual components; (2) the `Math.min()` tri-cap in `calculateBatteryFlow` where night usage often dominates, making `dailyBatteryDeliveredKwh` equal to night load regardless of efficiency; (3) the compensating trade-off where lower efficiency diverts more solar to the battery (reducing export credit) while also reducing discharge (increasing bill), with the export cap partially canceling the bill increase. Verified with numerical experiments in `scratch/battery-efficiency-test.js` and `scratch/export-cap-analysis.js`.
