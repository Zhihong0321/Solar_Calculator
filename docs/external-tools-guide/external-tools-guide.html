External Tools

What is it?

  • A place to share apps you built outside our system.
  • Everyone in the team can see and open these apps.

How to open it

  • Go to the Agent Dashboard.
  • Tap External Tools (side menu or tools grid).
  • The page opens on the List tab.

Reading the List

  • Tap a card to open that app in a new tab.
  • "by Name" = who published the app.
  • The grey tag = the app's category.
  • Pencil and trash buttons show only on your own apps.

1Publish your app

Steps

  • Tap the Submission tab.
  • Fill in Title — your app's name.
  • Pick a Category.
  • Write a Short Description — what it does.
  • Paste the URL — your app's link.
  • Tap Publish.
  • You go back to the List. Your app is there.

Categories

  • Sales - Competition — contests, rankings.
  • Sales - Tools & Report — tools and reports for sales.
  • Other — anything else.

Tips

  • All 4 fields are required.
  • URL must start with https:// (or http://).
  • Keep the description short — max 300 characters.

2Edit or delete your app

Edit

  • Find your app in the List.
  • Tap the pencil button.
  • The form opens with your details.
  • Change what you need.
  • Tap Save changes.
  • Changed your mind? Tap Cancel.

Delete

  • Tap the red trash button.
  • Tap OK to confirm.
  • Your app is removed from the list.

Rules

  • Only the owner can edit or delete an app.
  • Other people's apps: you can only open them.
  • Delete cannot be undone.
