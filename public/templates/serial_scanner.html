How to scan

1. Click Start Camera and grant camera permission.

2. Point the camera at the barcode/QR — keep it steady inside the dashed box.

3. Or click Upload Image and pick a photo of the code.

4. Every successful scan is added to the list on the right. Use Copy or Download CSV to export.
