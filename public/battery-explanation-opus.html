❓ Common doubts

Is the calculator hiding the loss?
No. Loss is fully tracked. At 20% loss the system pulls 17 kWh from solar to deliver 13.6 kWh — the 3.4 kWh gap is the loss. It just gets paid out of export credit, not your bill.
Why doesn't 20% loss = 20% less saving?
Because 20% loss only means you need 25% more solar input to deliver the same output. If surplus solar is available, output stays the same. Like a leaky pipe with a deep well — the bucket still fills.
So efficiency doesn't matter at all?
It does — but in a small way. Lower efficiency = less surplus left to export. Typical impact: RM 5–30 / month between best and worst battery.
What do I tell a doubting customer?
Show this page. The math is min(surplus, batterySize, nightUse ÷ efficiency). Efficiency only bites when it pushes the third term above the surplus. Standard engineering — not a trick.
𝑓 For the curious — actual formulas tap to open
# Efficiency RTE = 1 − loss% η = √RTE # per direction usable = size × (1 − DoD%) # Charge input (the 3 caps) input = min( surplus, usable / η, nightUse / RTE ) # Battery flow stored = input × η delivered = min(stored × η, nightUse) # Bill bill_offset = delivered × RM 0.49 export_credit = (surplus − input) × RM 0.21

When CAP C wins → delivered = nightUse, independent of η. That's why the bill stays flat.
